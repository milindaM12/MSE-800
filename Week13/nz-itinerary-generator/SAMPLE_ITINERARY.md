# Transport Plan

Use a rental car from Auckland to Wellington because it gives flexibility for
Waitomo, Rotorua, and Taupō. Return the vehicle in Wellington and fly to Queenstown
to avoid several additional days of driving. In Queenstown, use local buses,
walking, airport transfers, and organised tours rather than hiring another car.

# Practical New Zealand Travel Advice

- Drive on the left and allow more time than the distance alone suggests.
- Check weather and road conditions before every long drive.
- Check DOC alerts before visiting tracks, conservation land, or remote areas.
- Carry layers and waterproof clothing, even in summer.
- Mobile coverage can be limited outside cities and towns.
- Do not move soil, seeds, plants, food, or outdoor equipment through biosecurity
  controls without declaring them.
- Use refillable water bottles, stay on marked tracks, and follow local waste rules.
- Book popular summer accommodation, domestic flights, ferries, and tours early.
